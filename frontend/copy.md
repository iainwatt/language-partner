# Language Partner homepage copy

Current direction: standalone language practice app, 5 November 2026.
The rendered source is `src/content/landing.ts` and `src/app/page.tsx`.

1. **Learn languages. Naturally.** Talk about your day. Tell a story. Follow a
   thought. Language Partner gives you someone to practise with, and the words to
   keep going.
   Under the download badge, four examples rotate: “I want to talk about a problem
   I’m having with a colleague at work.”
   (Spanish), interview practice (French), a conversation with a teacher at a
   daughter’s school (Mandarin), and perfecting a leadership presentation
   (Russian). Each appears in a learner speech bubble with a voice waveform,
   followed by the AI listening, thinking, and giving a relevant follow-up:
   what happened at work, the interview role, what to discuss with the teacher,
   or the presentation’s opening and main idea. Both bubbles have English above
   the translation. Supporting line: “Example conversations. Talk about
   whatever’s on your mind.”
2. **You know more than you can say.** The gap between recognising a language and
   finding your own words, followed by an invitation to practise.
3. **Create. Save. Repeat.** An interactive, illustrative interview lesson journey.
   Create a specific brief covering business vocabulary, phrasal verbs and initial
   /h/ sounds. Save the conversation recap, four useful phrases, grammar focus and
   pronunciation practice words. Return to the same question with those words
   close by, and compare an earlier answer with a clearer one. No numerical
   improvement score or real learner results are claimed.
4. **A starting point. A conversation of your own.** An extensive library of
   grammar topics and role plays gives learners a place to begin, with room to
   bring their own emotions, opinions and experiences. Two interactive views
   show grammar becoming a personal story and a familiar scene becoming the
   learner’s own conversation. Six illustrative examples can be selected: past
   tense, future tense, conditionals, a school meeting, a landlord conversation
   and a restaurant. No catalogue counts are claimed.
5. **A conversation. With a little help along the way.** Free conversation about
   personally meaningful topics, with words and grammar support close by.
6. **Find a word. Make it yours.** Translate and save phrases, keep them beside
   the conversation, and return to recall practice.
7. **Know the rule. Get a feel for it.** Practise already-taught grammar using a
   short guide and a conversational prompt.
8. **Made for practice. By a language learner.** Iain’s story as a language teacher
   and software engineer who has learned several languages despite a “memory like
   a sieve.” Speaking brought the biggest improvements, but finding enough
   practice and a patient listener was hard. Existing apps felt too prescriptive
   and boring, leading him to build Language Partner. A warm portrait placeholder
   sits beside the story on desktop and above it on mobile.
9. **Your first 15 minutes. On us.** Planned one-time free conversation allowance,
   followed by a subscription. No paid price or allowance is confirmed yet.
10. **A few things you might be wondering.** A2+ audience, included AI access,
   speaking/typing, current language choices, private testing and connectivity.
11. **Make room for a little conversation.** A final invitation back to access.

No public App Store link has been supplied. The hero, trial section and closing
invitation show “Download on the App Store” badges that intentionally do nothing.
The FAQ retains the private-testing status. No addresses, payments or signup
confirmations are collected or fabricated.

The standalone Weekend plans phone preview below the hero slider is currently
omitted. The slider leads directly into “You know more than you can say.”

Product previews use illustrative Russian/English example content and are labelled
as examples. They never display the owner’s private chats. Founder copy is based
on Iain’s own account, supplied on 4 November 2026.
